# English at UCF

A one-page introduction to the Bachelor of Arts in English at the University of Central Florida, written for high school students considering UCF. It covers the three tracks (Creative Writing, Literature, Technical Communication), featured courses, departmental distinctions, careers, and how to apply.

The design follows the UCF Department of English website (black, white, and UCF gold; Montserrat and League Gothic as stand-ins for Gotham and Knockout).

## Sources

- [UCF Undergraduate Catalog, 2026–2027](https://www.ucf.edu/catalog/undergraduate/)
- [Department of English undergraduate programs](https://cah.ucf.edu/english/undergraduate/)
- [Department of English course schedule](https://cah.ucf.edu/english/courses/) (Summer 2026 – Spring 2027)

Featured courses are tied to specific semesters. Update them when the schedule changes.

## Publishing

The site is static HTML and CSS with no build step. To publish with GitHub Pages, go to **Settings → Pages**, set the source to **Deploy from a branch**, and choose `main` and `/ (root)`.
