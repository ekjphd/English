The Bachelor of Arts in English

Study creative writing, literature, or technical communication at the University of Central Florida, on campus or fully online.

Explore the tracks

Tracks

Every student earns a B.A. in English and chooses one of three tracks.

English B.A.Creative Writing

Write fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, and scripts. Students develop their craft in writing workshops and through close study of published work.

  • Fiction, Poetry, and Scriptwriting Workshops
  • Creative Nonfiction Workshop
  • Worldbuilding
  • The Florida Review
Program requirements

English B.A.Literature

Read discerningly, think critically, and write clearly. Students study literature in its historical, cultural, and theoretical contexts, from the Middle Ages to the present.

  • Shakespeare Studies
  • Gothic Literature
  • Literature and Law
  • Caribbean Literature
Program requirements

English B.A.Technical Communication

Write, design, and edit documents, websites, and visuals that make complex information clear. Students complete projects for real clients.

  • Visual Technical Communication
  • Usability Testing for Technical Communication
  • Writing for the Computer Industry
  • Client-Based Project Management
Program requirements

The department also offers seven minors, including Creative Writing, Linguistics, and Digital Humanities in the Age of AI. View all minors.

Featured Courses

A selection of courses on the department's 2026–2027 schedule.

ENL 3451, Spring 2027

Tolkien's Bookshelf

The poetry and legends that shaped Middle-earth, and how Tolkien drew on them to build his world.

LIT 3482, Spring 2027

Zombies: Undead Media from the Gothic to the Algorithm

The zombie in literature, film, and games, from Haitian Vodou narratives to post-apocalyptic fiction.

CRW 4125, Spring 2027

Worldbuilding

The class builds a world together, and each student writes a full-length project set in it.

CRW 3713, Fall 2026

Writing for Video Games

Game design and interactive storytelling. Students outline their own game and script its cut scenes.

ENG 3861, Spring 2027

Digital Humanities Approaches to AI

The history of artificial intelligence, its effect on culture and creativity, and its use in literary research.

LIN 4660, Spring 2027

Linguistics and Literature

Students research word histories for the Oxford English Dictionary and help build Noah Webster's Dictionary Online.

LIT 3931, Summer 2026

Viking Literature: Myths, Legends, and Sagas

Stories of Thor, Odin, and Loki, legendary heroes, and the sagas of the Icelanders.

ENL 4303, Spring 2027

British Authors: Jane Austen

All six of Austen's published novels, from Northanger Abbey to Persuasion.

Topics vary by semester. See the full course schedule.

Distinctions

Opportunities for undergraduates beyond the classroom.

Honors Undergraduate Thesis

Established in 1989, it is UCF's oldest undergraduate research program. Students write and defend an original thesis, which the UCF Libraries publish.

Learn more

The Florida Review

Students intern with UCF's literary journal. They evaluate submissions from working writers and help produce each issue.

Visit the journal

Study Abroad

The department has exchange programs with the University of Surrey in England and the University of Canberra in Australia.

Sigma Tau Delta

UCF's Zeta Xi chapter of the National English Honor Society has been active since 1980.
